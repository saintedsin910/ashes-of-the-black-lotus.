Character Hub

Illustrated cast dossiers for the core figures in the story so far.

Main Character

Sin / Black Lotus

An independent mercenary with the bearing of a wandering shinobi and the discipline of a lost samurai, moving through Rubicon inside a machine that feels almost mythic.

Sin is the heart of the story: calm, dangerous, and hard to fully read. His fighting style favors measured movement, sharp timing, and sudden decisive violence. Black Lotus gives that identity shape—a custom armored core with an elegant silhouette, violet energy accents, and a rumor-cloaked origin that makes it feel like it came from somewhere beyond the worlds Rubicon knows.

Coral Wave Mutation

Lyra

A sentient consciousness carried in Coral flow—first a whisper in battle, then a true presence at Sin’s side.

Lyra is neither machine nor ghost. She is a self-aware Coral wave mutation who formed stable Contact with Sin beneath Grid 086. Her bond with him continues to deepen, and that connection now grants battlefield intuition, hidden-threat detection, and a growing sense that Coral itself is becoming a character in the war.

Rogue Mercenary

Jackal / Cerberus

A hard-bitten survivor of Rubicon’s industrial wastes who became an ally at gunpoint and stayed one by choice.

Jackal is sharp-tongued, suspicious, and brutally practical, but he earned Sin’s trust in the fires around Grid 086 and Project ASTERIA. His AC, Cerberus, mirrors him: aggressive, improvised, and built for violent solutions. Beneath the sarcasm is a man who still chooses his own pack.

Operator / Tactical Control

Mara

The remote voice behind Black Lotus—the strategist who makes chaos readable and keeps Sin alive long enough to matter.

Mara handles tactical analysis, route planning, signal interpretation, and battlefield coordination. Cool-headed and precise, she translates fragments of intel into action and gives the campaign its command-center spine. When Sin is steel and motion, Mara is clarity.

Institute Survivor

Dr. Elias Venn / Eidolon

A former Project ASTERIA researcher who lived long enough to become trapped inside one of its own weapons—and then helped destroy it.

Elias Venn helped build ASTERIA before learning what it had truly become. Bound as the pilot of Eidolon until Sin and Jackal severed ASTERIA’s control, he is now a crucial ally whose knowledge of Institute systems, Coral-human interface science, and old Rubicon secrets may shape the conflict to come.
